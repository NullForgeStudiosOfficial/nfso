What's your general philosophy when designing a game?
1. Take cool idea 2. compare it agains the universes lore.  2a. Does it make sense in the lore? → if yes: Move onto 3, If not: Scrap that shit. 3. Does it fit the game genre?  3a. Objectively? If no: scrap, If yes: move to 3b  3b. Subjectively? Will a majority people like this addition? If yes: move to 4, if no: move to 3c.   3c. ok. Fuck people. Will a niche group of people appriciate this idea? If no. Scrap it. If yes. Move to 4. 4. What systems does it interact with?   4a. After determining what it interacts with. consider work load. If its massive. scrap, if its not move to 5. 5. Temper priority. move to 5a  5a. what needs done first before implementation? the answer to this might affect 4a. re-evaluate at implementation time. if its high priority. move to 6. else. wait for re-evaluation. 6.If this was implemented. Would taking it out of the game make the game actively worse?  6a. If yes, Keep it, Move to 7. If no. reconsider. go back to 3. 7. put cool idea into game.

~Dev Questions~
What makes a game good to you?
Give me a mission. I need quests, goals, and something attainable to work toward. I love being able to wander off, explore, collect things, decorate, and get completely distracted, but eventually I need that little quest marker calling me home. Give me freedom, but give that freedom a purpose.
What's a game mechanic or design trend you can't stand?
NOT BEING ABLE TO SAVE WHENEVER THE FUCK I WANT.
What's a game mechanic you'll defend until the day you die?
OPTIONS. Give me options. Let me customize my character, choose how I play, decide where I go, pick what I wear, decorate things, make choices, and generally stick my grubby little hands into the game and make it mine. Even if the choice changes absolutely nothing mechanically, I still want the choice.
~Personal Questions~
What's something that will instantly put you in a good mood?
Are you a morning person, night person, or some forbidden third thing?
Some forbidden third thing. I love the wee hours of the morning, when the world hasn't quite awakened yet. When the morning glories slowly open their petals and everything is still jeweled with dew. There's something peaceful about those hours, when the air is cool and quiet and it feels like you've caught the world before everyone else gets their hands on it. But I love the night just as much. I love the stars, the cool breeze, the darkness, and the way everything settles when the day finally lets go. I love sitting outside when the world has gone quiet and looking up at a sky that suddenly feels impossibly large. So I don't think I'm a morning person or a night person. I belong to the hours when everyone else is asleep.
What's your ideal way to spend a completely free day?
No plans. No obligations. Nowhere I have to be. I want to wake up when I wake up, have something good to drink, play games, watch something, wander outside if the weather is nice, and spend time with the people I love. The important part is getting to decide what happens next instead of the clock deciding for me.
What's something people tend to misunderstand about you?
My intensity. I feel things loudly. I love loudly, get excited loudly, get angry loudly, and sometimes I just exist loudly. That doesn't mean every emotion is a crisis or that I'm asking someone else to fix it. Sometimes I just need to feel the damn thing.
What's the fastest way to become your friend?
Be weird with me. Skip the small talk. Tell me your bizarre theory. Show me the thing you're ridiculously excited about. Send me the meme you weren't sure you should send. Let me see who you actually are instead of presenting your customer-service personality.  • Bonus points if you're funny and don't require constant maintenance to remain friends.
What's something you're embarrassingly sentimental about?
Little things that have memories attached to them. A note, a stupid little trinket, a rock somebody handed me, something my kids made, some completely worthless object that reminds me of a person or a moment. Once something has a story attached to it, good fucking luck getting me to throw it away.
What's a hill you'll die on that ultimately doesn't fucking matter?
My current hill I'll die on: The entire plot of 101 Dalmatians. It could have been avoided if Roger and Anita had just lied to Cruella and told her the puppies were white. Cruella shows up asking about the puppies and their spots, and instead of using this magnificent opportunity to get that woman the fuck away from their dogs, they explain that Dalmatian puppies are born white and develop their spots later. WHY WOULD YOU TELL HER THAT? She doesn't need a lesson in Dalmatian biology. She needs misinformation. “Terribly disappointing, Cruella. Fifteen completely white puppies. Not a spot among them. Weirdest damn Dalmatians we've ever seen.” That's it. She loses interest. Puppies remain unskinned. Movie over. This could have been a twelve-minute film.
What's something popular that you just don't understand the appeal of?
A lot of new trends, honestly. Half the time I see something suddenly become wildly popular and my first response is just, “But... why though?” Not even necessarily because I hate it. I just need someone to explain how we collectively arrived here. Sometimes I eventually understand. Sometimes I join in. And sometimes I remain standing on the shore watching everyone sail away on their weird little trend boat thinking, “No, seriously. Why?”

I can be both. Two seemingly contradictory things can be true about me at the same time. I can love you and need space. I can be afraid and still want to try. I can be happy and still have something hurting beneath it. I feel things deeply, and those feelings don't always arrive one at a time.

Curious, loud, sentimental, stubborn, imaginative, slightly feral, and perpetually distracted by something interesting. Lore. Completely unnecessary lore. Give me the history, the weird little fact, why something is the way it is, where a tradition came from, what happened behind the scenes. What's something you absolutely can't stand?
People telling me how I feel or deciding what I meant without asking me. I'm crass. I'm loud. I swear a lot, and sometimes the way I say something sounds harsher or more serious than I actually mean it. If you're unsure what I meant, ask me. What's something you're irrationally good at?
Connecting completely unrelated dots. My brain collects useless little details and stores them in some unknowable filing system until, without warning, two things click together and suddenly they make sense.
